Common Questions

Engagement FAQ

How does the Audit fee credit toward a Full Build work? expand_more

The 2-Week Workflow & Capacity Audit is a fixed-fee engagement that gives you a complete technical specification and your first working automation prototype. If you decide to kick off a Full Capacity & System Build within 30 days of completing the Audit, 100% of your Audit fee is credited directly toward the build cost.

Why do clients stay on the monthly Continuous Ops Partner retainer after the build? expand_more

Agencies and consultancies are dynamic: new hires join, billable targets change, new client templates are needed, and additional workflow bottlenecks surface once the core capacity planner is live. Rather than selling passive bug-fix insurance, our monthly partnership calibrates your capacity model every month and ships new workflow automations from your backlog on a predictable cadence.

Do you partner with Fractional CFOs or CRM implementation agencies? expand_more

Yes. We frequently partner with Fractional Agency CFOs (who need clean, automated utilization and capacity data from their clients' delivery teams) and HubSpot/Salesforce consultants (who want a trusted engineering partner to handle post-sale Google Workspace and delivery handoff builds).
