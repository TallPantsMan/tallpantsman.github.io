Resource Planning at Scale

37 People

~16 Hrs/Mo
Total Org Savings
~590 Hrs/Mo

Challenge: A growing multi-team organization managed project schedules, headcount, and client assignments across isolated spreadsheets. Updates had to be chased down manually each week, data frequently fell out of sync, and department heads lacked clear visibility into who was available for upcoming work.

What We Built: A centralized capacity and allocation system built natively inside Google Workspace using Google Apps Script. It tied team rosters directly into weekly allocation sheets, automated rollups across departments, enforced view permissions by role, and provided managers with an up-to-date availability dashboard.

Outcome: Deployed across 17 teams and 37 staff members. The client estimated that reducing back-and-forth scheduling messages and manual spreadsheet maintenance saved roughly 16 hours per person each month, totaling about 590 hours saved across the company monthly.

* Note: Delivered via our extended network on a large enterprise engagement. Hours saved are client internal estimates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why build inside Google Workspace instead of buying specialized resource software?

Many organizations already pay for Google Workspace and have teams comfortable working in Sheets. Building custom logic on top of Apps Script provides a tailored solution directly within their daily interface, without requiring additional per-seat licensing. When standard market tools don't map cleanly to your internal process, building natively often leads to faster team adoption.
