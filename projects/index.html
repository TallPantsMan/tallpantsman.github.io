Google Workspace Automation Resource Planning Enterprise Rollout

Resource Planning at Scale

Challenge: A large organization managed team capacity and project assignments through manual, spreadsheet-based processes — slow to update, easy to get wrong, and invisible to leadership until something broke.

System Built: A custom resource management system, built natively in Google Workspace using Google Apps Script. Features centralized capacity modeling, automated cross-team status synchronization, role-based access control, and real-time leadership dashboards.

Result: The system was rolled out across 17 teams and 37 people. Based on internal estimates, it gave back roughly 16 hours a month per person — about 590 hours a month across the org — previously spent on manual scheduling and status tracking.

* Note: Built via our extended network on a large enterprise engagement. Numbers are internal estimates, not third-party audited.

What results has CaulHaus delivered in enterprise process automation?

What technology stack does CaulHaus use for enterprise resource planning?

CaulHaus builds lightweight, native enterprise tools inside existing cloud stacks like Google Workspace (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets API) and custom integration layers.
