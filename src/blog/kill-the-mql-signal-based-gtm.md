# Kill The MQL: Signal-Based GTM

Stop obsessing over MQLs. Your static lead scoring model is filling the pipeline with garbage. Broad, volume-based outreach captures engagement that rarely translates into actual revenue. 

You need to focus on the 5% of the market that is actively buying right now. 

That means shifting to Signal-Based GTM. Instead of relying on demographic assumptions, you react to real-time intent data. You track actual behavior. 

## Defining the Signal

A signal is a concrete data point proving a target account is ready to buy. We look at three layers:

- **First-Party:** High-intent website visits, content downloads, and product usage telemetry.
- **Second-Party:** Activity within partner ecosystems or review sites like G2. 
- **Third-Party:** External intent data, funding rounds, and executive job changes.

Do not confuse intent with fit. A fit signal just means an account matches your Ideal Customer Profile. They have the right employee count or tech stack. An intent signal means they are bleeding and need a bandage immediately. They are repeatedly viewing your pricing page. You need both to close a deal, but intent dictates the timing.

## It's A RevOps Problem

This isn't a marketing tactic. It's a systems architecture challenge. 

If your CRM, marketing automation, and intent data tools don't talk to each other perfectly, you fail. Your RevOps team has to filter the noise so Sales only sees actionable insights. 

Clean data and robust API integrations are non-negotiable. Without them, critical buying signals rot in departmental silos. Your pipeline velocity dies.

## Building the Engine

You can't buy a single tool to fix this. You have to align sales, marketing, and operations. 

### Start Small
Analyze your closed-won data. Pick two or three behavioral actions that consistently predicted true buying intent. Ignore the rest of the noise. 

### Automate the Playbook
Map out automated responses for those primary signals. If a past product champion takes a new job at a target account, your account executive should be notified instantly with context for outreach. 

### Force Alignment
Stop measuring gross email volume. Shift the entire revenue organization's metrics to pipeline velocity and conversion rates tied to signal-triggered engagement. 

## The Reality

Clinging to outdated MQL scoring will cost you deals. Engaging buyers based on real-time intent is the only way to scale effectively.
