01 Data Controller & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Caul Haus Group, Inc. (“CaulHaus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you visit our website at https://caulhaus.com (“Site”), submit an inquiry, request an operational audit, or communicate with us.

Data Controller Contact:

Caul Haus Group, Inc. • Attention: Alex Herbstman, Founder & Systems Architect

Inquiries & Privacy Requests: alex@caulhaus.com

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of personal information necessary to evaluate your operational requirements and provide professional consulting services:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you complete our contact or audit request form, or email us directly, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your full name and work email address.
  • Organization Details: Your company name, company website URL, and primary delivery software tools.
  • Operational Scope & Project Description: The operational bottlenecks, project goals, team size, or questions you submit in freeform text.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you navigate our Site, our hosting and content delivery infrastructure (GitHub Pages) automatically records standard web request headers in server logs:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate geographic location derived from IP.
  • Browser type, browser version, operating system, and device category.
  • Date and time stamps, referring URLs, and requested pages.

This technical data is used strictly for server health, DDoS mitigation, and website security, and is never combined with personally identifiable contact records.

03 Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following lawful grounds established under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • check_circle Consent: When you voluntarily submit your contact details via our inquiry form requesting a response or audit.
  • check_circle Contractual Necessity: To take preparatory steps prior to entering into a 2-Week Workflow & Capacity Audit or Full System Build agreement, and to perform client delivery services.
  • check_circle Legitimate Interests: To operate, maintain, and secure our website, prevent abuse or spam submissions, and manage standard business-to-business communications.
  • check_circle Legal Obligations: To comply with statutory tax, financial accounting, and regulatory recordkeeping duties.

04 How We Use Your Information

We use collected personal information solely for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule discovery calls, and scope operational audits.
  • To prepare tailored architectural proposals, statements of work, and diagnostic roadmaps.
  • To provide hands-on delivery operations engineering, capacity planning software builds, and ongoing retainer support.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent activity, security vulnerabilities, or spam submissions.

• Strict No-Spam & Non-Compete Guarantee: We do not add your contact information to automated third-party marketing lists, nor do we sell or disclose client engagement details in violation of mutual NDAs or covenants.

05 Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure

We share information only with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing form transmissions, and conducting business operations. All providers are subject to strict confidentiality and security covenants:

Web3Forms

Transmits contact form submissions securely to our inbox via encrypted API.

GitHub Pages

Hosts static website assets and provides global edge CDN caching and security.

Google Workspace

Provides secure business email hosting, calendar scheduling, and cloud documentation.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

CaulHaus operates a privacy-first web architecture. We do not use tracking cookies, remarketing pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics (such as Meta Pixel, TikTok, or Google Ads Remarketing tags).

Our website operates entirely without requiring advertising cookie consent banners because we do not deploy non-essential tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time with zero impact on your ability to browse our content or submit contact inquiries.

07 Data Retention & Security

We retain personal contact information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy—typically for the duration of a client relationship, plus statutory accounting and tax retention periods (up to 7 years for financial records). Inquiries that do not materialize into an active engagement are periodically purged.

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all site transit, encrypted form delivery, multi-factor authentication (MFA) across operational accounts, and role-based access restrictions.

09 California & US State Privacy Disclosures

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and equivalent state laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and Texas:

  • Notice at Collection: We collect contact identifiers and professional organizational information strictly for direct communication and audit delivery.
  • No Sale or Sharing: CaulHaus does not sell your personal information, nor do we share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months.
  • No Sensitive Personal Information: We do not collect or process sensitive personal information (such as financial account credentials, government identifiers, or precise geolocation).
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you in pricing, service availability, or response quality for exercising any statutory privacy right.

10 Children’s Online Privacy Protection

Our website, content, and services are exclusively intended for professional B2B organizations and individuals aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 16. If we become aware that an individual under 16 has submitted personal information, we will delete that data immediately.
