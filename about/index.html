Operating Principles

Build what your team actually uses.

Complex systems fail when they don't fit how people actually work. We talk directly with the people running each process to build automations that integrate smoothly into their daily habits.

table_view

Graduate core workflows out of manual workbooks.

Spreadsheets work well for individual analysis, but managing multi-team handoffs inside them leads to version confusion and copy-paste errors. We connect your systems with dependable scripts and APIs so records update automatically.

You work directly with the developers designing and writing your automations from the first call through rollout, keeping feedback loops tight and requirements accurate.

Rather than introducing unfamiliar platforms that require team retraining, we build custom logic inside the tools you already rely on: Google Workspace, your CRM, and standard webhooks.

Common Questions

About CaulHaus: FAQ

Who runs CaulHaus?

CaulHaus is an operations and automation studio led by founder Alex Herbstman. Alex leads every project directly to diagnose bottlenecks and build core integrations, collaborating with a trusted bench of senior specialists for niche software platforms and additional development capacity.

What kind of projects do you take on?

We build custom operational automations: linking disparate tools, building Google Apps Script tools inside Sheets and Drive, routing CRM leads, and replacing fragile spreadsheets with dependable scripts.

What size teams do you work with?

Most clients are 20 to 150 person agencies, consultancies, implementation partners, and multi-team organizations running on Google Workspace that have outgrown manual staffing and status spreadsheets—without wanting the per-seat cost and adoption friction of bloated PSA software.

What deliverables do we receive at the end of an engagement?

You receive working code and automated workflows tested on your production data, accompanied by clear documentation and video walkthroughs so your team can maintain and adapt them independently.
