How We Work: FAQ

Who is CaulHaus?

CaulHaus is an operations architecture and workflow automation consultancy. We architect cross-functional operational infrastructure and build the custom automation software required to eliminate spreadsheet bottlenecks, offering direct founder engineering backed by scalable network capacity.

What does an operations architect at CaulHaus do?

An operations architect at CaulHaus analyzes your cross-tool workflows, eliminates manual spreadsheet handoffs, and builds custom automation across UX operations, vendor management, marketing operations, capacity planning, and revenue operations.

Who should hire an operations architecture consultant?

Growing businesses and enterprise teams scaling beyond 5 to 50+ individuals who are constrained by manual spreadsheets, fragmented handoffs, or disconnected toolchains should hire an operations architect to establish robust, automated systems.

What does CaulHaus do differently?

Unlike traditional management consultancies that only provide strategic advice without implementation, or generic developers who lack deep operational insight, CaulHaus designs the complete operational architecture and writes the production code to run it.

Tell us what is slowing your team down. We will map out a clear plan to resolve your operational bottlenecks and automate your workflows.
