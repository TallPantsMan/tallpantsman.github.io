01 Our Commitment

Caul Haus Group, Inc. (“CaulHaus”) is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We believe the web should be accessible, usable, and inclusive for everyone, and we continually audit and improve the user experience of our website in compliance with relevant accessibility standards.

02 Conformance Standards

To ensure an accessible experience, CaulHaus adheres to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), and aligns with the requirements of Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

These guidelines define how to make web content more accessible to individuals with a wide range of visual, auditory, motor, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

03 Key Measures Implemented

We incorporate accessibility into our design system, markup, and engineering practices across caulhaus.com:

Semantic HTML & Landmarks

We use valid, semantic HTML5 elements including proper landmark roles (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>, <article>) to allow screen reader users to navigate logically between sections.

Keyboard Navigation & Skip Links

All interactive navigation links, dropdown menus, and form fields are operable via standard keyboard controls (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space). We provide a direct “Skip to main content” link at the very top of each page for rapid keyboard navigation.

Color Contrast & Typography

Our architectural palette is tuned to satisfy or exceed the WCAG AA minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display headers. Text size and spacing can be adjusted by user browsers up to 200% without loss of layout integrity.

Form Accessibility & Clear Labels

All inquiry form fields feature explicit, visible <label> elements with matching id and for attributes, distinct error outlines, and standard ARIA markup.

Text Alternatives & Motion Controls

Informational graphics and team images include descriptive alt text. Background visual shaders are marked with decorative roles and do not convey essential operational data.

04 Technical Specifications

Accessibility of the CaulHaus website relies on the following technologies to work with the particular combination of web browser and any assistive technologies or plugins installed on your computer:

  • HTML5
  • WAI-ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications)
  • CSS3 / Tailwind CSS
  • Modern JavaScript (ECMAScript 2020+)
