Common Questions

Process Automation FAQ

When does custom Google Workspace automation make sense versus commercial SaaS?

Commercial SaaS products excel at standardized, out-of-the-box workflows. However, for specialized internal handoffs and proprietary operational steps, custom Apps Script tools avoid per-seat recurring fees and adapt directly to your team's existing workflow and domain permissions.

What types of automations do you build?

Resource allocation dashboards, live workload planners, multi-department approval triggers, automated report generation, and API integrations that connect your CRM, spreadsheets, and Slack.

Who maintains the automation scripts after deployment?

We write clean, modular, and thoroughly documented code meant for internal self-sufficiency. We provide technical walkthroughs and reference docs for your team, alongside ongoing on-call support from Alex and our engineering bench for new features or maintenance.
