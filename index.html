Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to buy new software? keyboard_arrow_down
No. The goal is to make your existing tools do the heavy lifting. We build automations inside what you already pay for, such as Google Workspace, your CRM, and project management tools.
What kinds of tasks do you automate? keyboard_arrow_down
Routine operational work: copying data between spreadsheets, assigning new leads to sales reps, sending renewal reminders, syncing customer records across apps, and compiling weekly status summaries.
How long does a project take? keyboard_arrow_down
An initial systems review takes two weeks. A full build (connecting tools, writing scripts, testing edge cases, and training your team) typically takes four to six weeks.
Who actually builds the automations? keyboard_arrow_down
Founder Alex Herbstman leads every project, conducts the initial technical review, and writes the core automations. For niche platforms, complex database migrations, or specialized requirements, Alex pulls directly from a vetted bench of senior specialists. You collaborate directly with technical builders throughout the engagement.
