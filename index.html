Process Blueprint

How We Build Your Systems

Good systems require careful planning first. Here's our three-step process to get everything running smoothly.

01

Audit & Map

We look at how your marketing and operations actually run today, not how the org chart says they run, and find where time, leads, or information are getting lost.

02

Design & Build

We build the strategy, the campaigns, or the automation itself. Most of what we ship is working software or a running process, not just a recommendation deck.

03

Support & Improve

Systems drift. We stay attached long enough to fix what breaks, tune what's underperforming, and hand off something your team can actually maintain.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should hire a marketing ops consultant? keyboard_arrow_down
We work best with growing teams (5-50+ people) who are drowning in manual spreadsheets, endless status updates, or disconnected tools. If your team spends more time managing work than doing it, it's time to automate those repetitive workflows.
What does CaulHaus do differently? keyboard_arrow_down
Unlike standard marketing agencies that only hand over strategy decks without execution bandwidth, or generic software developers without marketing experience, we design the strategy and build the custom software layer that runs it.
What software and tools does CaulHaus automate or integrate? keyboard_arrow_down
CaulHaus builds custom automated workflows primarily in Google Workspace (Google Apps Script), CRM systems (HubSpot, Salesforce), multi-channel attribution engines (GA4, Looker Studio), and integration tools like Make or Zapier.
How long does a systems audit or build take? keyboard_arrow_down
Our Systems & Strategy Audit is completed in 2 weeks. Strategy and system builds typically take between 4 to 6 weeks depending on team size and tool integration requirements.
