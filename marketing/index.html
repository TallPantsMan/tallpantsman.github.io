Handoff Bottlenecks

Sales-to-Delivery Problems We Solve

01. Messy Closed-Won to Delivery Handoffs keyboard_arrow_down
When sales closes a deal in HubSpot or Salesforce, PMs shouldn't have to chase down SOW details, manually build Drive folders, or copy hours into a staffing sheet. We trigger complete project spin-up and resource requests automatically the second a deal is marked Closed-Won.
02. Blindness Between Sales Pipeline & Delivery Capacity keyboard_arrow_down
Sales often closes deals for roles that are already at 110% utilization. By syncing late-stage CRM deals directly into your Google Workspace capacity planner, leadership sees upcoming staffing crunches weeks before contracts are signed.
03. Slow Inbound Lead Follow-Up & Attribution Gaps keyboard_arrow_down
High-value prospective clients sitting in a shared inbox lose momentum fast. We link your web forms, CRM, and Slack so incoming inquiries reach the right partner or account lead in seconds, with clean source attribution tied all the way to closed revenue.
